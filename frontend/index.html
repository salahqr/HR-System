How it works

A four-step attendance verification process for everyday workplace operations.

1

Look at the door camera

Approach the entrance sensor for facial verification.

2

Door screen confirms

The display confirms verification and access status.

3

Status calculated automatically

Present or late status is calculated against your schedule.

4

See results on dashboard
